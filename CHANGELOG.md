# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-30

First release.

- Five pages — home, about, services, contact, FAQ — plus a branded 404/500 page.
- The treatment menu on `/services`, as four tabs: Featured, Men's Sugaring,
  Bundles and Intimate. Every price, duration and description is transcribed
  from the live Fresha booking menu.
- Contact form, posting to FormSubmit.
- Full-bleed dark theme with the studio's brand marks, EB Garamond throughout.
- Server-rendered and prerendered to static HTML, with per-page titles,
  descriptions, canonicals and Open Graph tags, LocalBusiness and FAQPage
  structured data, and a generated `sitemap.xml`.
- English only.
